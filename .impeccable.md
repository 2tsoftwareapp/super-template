## Design Context

### Users
Internal product and engineering teams using the web app as a production-ready starter.
They need a workspace that makes authentication, permissions, CRUD flows, and admin surfaces feel coherent and easy to scan.

### Brand Personality
Structured, calm, and precise.
The interface should feel dependable rather than flashy: a modern control room with clear hierarchy and very little visual noise.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-first enterprise UI with warm neutral surfaces, disciplined spacing, and subtle contrast.
The tone should be editorial and production-oriented, not playful or decorative.
Use shadcn/ui as the visual system backbone and avoid bespoke chrome where primitives already solve the problem.

### Design Principles
- Prefer clear page headers, section cards, and visible actions over dense all-in-one layouts.
- Use shadcn primitives for form controls, lists, tables, badges, and empty states.
- Keep the palette restrained and warm with crisp emphasis on primary actions.
- Make auth, dashboard, and CRUD pages read as one product family.
- Preserve strong readability on both desktop and mobile.
